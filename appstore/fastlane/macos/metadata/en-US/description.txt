Flowie is a keyboard-first outliner for Mac. Plan a trip, take research notes or keep a journal. Open any bullet as its own page when you want to focus on one part of the outline.

WRITE AND ORGANIZE
• Nest, indent, collapse and drag bullets
• Headings, to-dos, numbered lists, quotes and code blocks
• Format text with keyboard shortcuts, the / menu or a right-click
• Paste or drop images into the outline and add captions
• Write inline or display LaTeX math with a live preview

CONNECT YOUR IDEAS
• Type [[ to link to any bullet. Hover over a link for a preview, or open the linked page to see its backlinks
• Mirror a bullet in several places and edit it from any of them
• Tags and mentions with completion
• Daily journal pages and a calendar

SEARCH AND JUMP
• ⌘P to jump to any bullet or create a new page
• Filter by tag, date, completion or recent changes
• Star pages, save searches and find unread imports with is:new

KEEP, SYNC AND SHARE YOUR NOTES
• Notes and images stay on your Mac, with no Flowie account. Work offline whenever you like
• Optional sync: keep the outline the same on your Macs through your own private iCloud database (macOS 14 or later), or through a Flowie server you run yourself
• Share a branch and its images as a .flowie file. The recipient can preview it, choose where to import it, and merge later updates while keeping their own edits
• Import and export Markdown, plain text and OPML, including imports from Workflowy. Flowie's OPML exports keep internal links and mirrors
• Restore deleted bullets from the Trash for up to 30 days
• Use the built-in tutorial to get started, adapt an example or look up a feature

AI ASSISTANTS
• Connect Claude or another MCP client. Choose Off, Read only or Read & write in Settings. You can undo each edit an assistant makes

Customizable shortcuts. Available in English and Simplified Chinese.
