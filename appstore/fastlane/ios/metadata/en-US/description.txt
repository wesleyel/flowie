Flowie keeps your plans, research notes, lists and daily journal in one outline. Tap the dot beside a bullet to open that branch as a page.

WRITE AND ORGANIZE
• Nest bullets as deep as you like. Use the toolbar above the keyboard to indent, outdent or move a row
• Fold branches, check off tasks and hide completed items
• Move a whole branch at once. Choose “Move To…” and type part of its destination’s name
• Add a note to any bullet. Undo or redo your edits

FOCUS ON ONE PAGE
• See where a page fits in the outline from the path beneath its title
• On iPad in landscape, keep the project on the left and open a detail beside it
• Star the pages you use most

JOURNAL AND QUICK CAPTURE
• Open Today to write in your journal, or step back to an earlier day
• Start writing from the Home Screen, Control Center or the Action Button on a supported iPhone
• Add a line with Siri or Shortcuts, or share text and web links to your journal from other apps

SEARCH
• Search the whole outline by word, #tag or date. Try is:todo to find tasks or changed:7d to revisit recent edits

YOUR OUTLINE, ON YOUR DEVICE
• Write and search offline on your iPhone or iPad without a Flowie account
• When signed in to iCloud, sync your bullets, notes and images between devices. To include your Mac, choose iCloud in the Mac App Store version of Flowie
• Restore deleted bullets from Recently Deleted

Available in English and Simplified Chinese.
