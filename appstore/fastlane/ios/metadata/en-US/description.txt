Flowie keeps everything in one outline of nested bullets: plans, research notes, lists and a daily journal. Tap any bullet to open it as its own page when you want to focus on one part.

WRITE AND ORGANIZE
• Nest bullets as deep as you like. Indent, outdent and move them from the toolbar above the keyboard
• Collapse and expand branches, and complete to-dos or hide them
• Move a bullet anywhere with Move to…, which finds the place as you type
• Add a note to any bullet
• Undo and redo every change

FOCUS ON ONE PAGE
• Tap a bullet to open it as a page. The path back to Home shows under its title
• Star the pages you use most

JOURNAL AND QUICK CAPTURE
• Today opens on today's journal page. Go back a day or forward a day
• Write in today's journal from the Home Screen, Control Center or the Action Button
• Add to today's journal with Siri or Shortcuts without opening the app

SEARCH
• Search the whole outline with words, #tags, dates and filters like is:todo or changed:7d

YOUR OUTLINE, ON YOUR DEVICE
• Your outline is kept on your iPhone or iPad and works offline. No account needed
• Sync your bullets and notes with Flowie on your Mac through iCloud. Images aren't synced through iCloud
• Restore deleted bullets from Recently Deleted

Text formatting, tags, dates and links show as they do on the Mac. Available in English and Simplified Chinese.
