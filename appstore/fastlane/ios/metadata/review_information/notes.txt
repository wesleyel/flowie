Flowie works on a single iPhone or iPad without a Flowie account, a Mac or an internet connection. You can use these steps offline.

1. Open Today from the tab bar on iPhone or from the sidebar on a wide iPad. Tap below the last entry and type “Trail notes #hike”. Press Return, type “Pack a map” and use Indent above the keyboard to make it a child of the first bullet.
2. Tap the dot beside “Trail notes #hike” to open its page. In landscape on iPad, the journal and the new page can appear side by side. Open Search and enter #hike to find the entry across the outline.
3. For quick capture, touch and hold the Flowie icon on the Home Screen and choose “Write in Today’s Journal”. To try the share extension, share text or a web link to Flowie, tap “Add to Today”, then open Today in Flowie.

Settings is under Home → … → Settings on iPhone and in the sidebar on a wide iPad. It shows Recently Deleted and the iCloud sync status. If the device is signed in to iCloud, Flowie syncs the outline through the user's private CloudKit database.
