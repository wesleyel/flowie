# Privacy Policy
{: #privacy-en}

Last updated and effective: 2026-11-18
{: .updated}

Flowie does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal information.
{: .lead}

- **Data collected: none.** Flowie's App Store privacy label is "Data Not Collected", and the app's privacy manifest declares no collected data and no tracking.
- **Your notes stay on your Mac.** Everything you write is saved in a database inside Flowie's app container on your Mac. Flowie has no accounts, no cloud sync, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting, and it makes no network requests of its own.
- **No third parties.** Flowie contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking code, and it shares nothing with third parties.
- **Files you choose.** When you import or export, Flowie reads or writes only the file you pick in the Open or Save dialog.
- **Links.** Clicking a web link opens it in your default browser; what happens there is governed by that website's policy.
- **AI agents (optional).** If you connect an AI assistant (for example Claude) through Flowie's MCP server, the assistant can read — and, if you allow it in Settings → Agent, change — your outline. That access goes through a local connection on your Mac only. What the assistant does with the content it reads is governed by that assistant's provider. You can turn agent access off at any time.
- **Deleting your data.** Bullets you delete go to Flowie's Trash (**File → Trash**) and are removed from it after 30 days. To delete everything Flowie has stored, quit Flowie and delete the folder `~/Library/Containers/flowie.spokey.cn`; moving the app to the Trash alone may leave that folder in place.
- **Children.** Flowie collects no data from anyone, including children.
{: .terms}

# 隐私政策
{: #privacy-zh}

更新及生效日期：2026-11-18
{: .updated}

Flowie 不收集、不在任何服务器上存储、不分享也不出售任何个人信息。
{: .lead}

- **收集的数据：无。** Flowie 在 App Store 上的隐私标签为「未收集数据」，应用的隐私清单也声明不收集任何数据、不进行追踪。
- **笔记只保存在你的 Mac 上。** 你写的一切都保存在 Flowie 应用容器内的数据库中。Flowie 没有账号、云同步、统计分析、广告或崩溃上报，自身不发起任何网络请求。
- **没有第三方。** Flowie 不包含任何第三方统计、广告或追踪代码，也不与任何第三方共享数据。
- **你选择的文件。** 导入或导出时，Flowie 只读取或写入你在打开 / 保存对话框中选择的文件。
- **链接。** 点击网页链接会在默认浏览器中打开，之后的行为受该网站的政策约束。
- **AI 助手（可选）。** 如果你通过 Flowie 的 MCP 服务器连接 AI 助手（例如 Claude），该助手可以读取你的大纲；在「设置 → Agent」中允许后还可以修改。这种访问只通过你 Mac 上的本地连接进行。助手如何处理所读取的内容，受其提供方的政策约束。你可以随时关闭助手访问。
- **删除你的数据。** 删除的条目会进入 Flowie 的废纸篓（**「文件 → 废纸篓」**），30 天后从中移除。要删除 Flowie 保存的全部数据，请退出 Flowie，然后删除文件夹 `~/Library/Containers/flowie.spokey.cn`；只把应用移到废纸篓可能会保留该文件夹。
- **儿童。** Flowie 不从任何人（包括儿童）收集数据。
{: .terms}

本政策如有变更，新版本会发布在本页面并更新日期。
