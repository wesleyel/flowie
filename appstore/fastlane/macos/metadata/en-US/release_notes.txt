Flowie 1.1 adds sync. Choose iCloud in Settings → Sync to keep your outline the same on your Macs through your private iCloud database (macOS 14 or later), or sync through a Flowie server you run yourself.

• Bullets, images, stars and saved searches sync, including edits made offline. When two Macs change the same text at once, the other version is kept as a conflict copy you can restore.
• Limit how wide the text grows in a wide window: Settings → Appearance or ⌃⌘W.
• Find a shortcut by name or keys in Settings → Shortcuts.
• Code blocks export as fenced code and import back as code. Markdown articles with headings import with their structure.
• Long outlines scroll more steadily, large branches fold without stutter, and undo history uses far less memory.

The first launch upgrades your outline's database and keeps a backup beside it.
