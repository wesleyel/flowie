Flowie is a fast, keyboard-first outliner for thinking in lists. Every bullet can be a page: zoom in to focus, zoom out to see the big picture.

WRITE AND ORGANIZE
• Nest, indent, collapse and drag bullets with smooth animations
• Headings, to-dos, numbered lists, quotes and code blocks
• Bold, italic, highlight, colors and links — from the keyboard, the / menu or a right-click
• Math with LaTeX syntax, rendered as you type

CONNECT YOUR IDEAS
• Link to any bullet with [[ and see backlinks on its page
• Mirrors: one bullet in several places, edits show everywhere
• Tags and mentions with completion
• Daily journal pages and a calendar

FIND ANYTHING
• ⌘P to jump to any bullet or create a new page
• Search with filters: tags, dates, completed, changed in the last days
• Star pages and save searches

YOUR DATA, ON YOUR MAC
• Everything is stored locally; nothing is sent anywhere
• Import OPML, Markdown and text (Workflowy exports work)
• Export Markdown, OPML or plain text
• Undo for everything, and a Trash that keeps deleted bullets for 30 days

FOR AI AGENTS
• A built-in MCP server lets AI assistants such as Claude read your outline — and edit it if you allow it in Settings. Off, read-only or read & write; every agent change is one undo step.

Every shortcut can be changed. English and Simplified Chinese.
